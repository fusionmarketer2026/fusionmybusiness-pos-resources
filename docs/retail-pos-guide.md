# Retail POS Software: An Introduction

Retail point-of-sale (POS) software helps shops process purchases and maintain records of sales.

## Common capabilities to evaluate

- Product and item management
- Barcode scanning support
- Payment processing
- Returns and refunds
- Sales reports
- Staff permissions
- Hardware compatibility

The exact features and integrations differ between providers. Verify requirements and supported functionality before choosing a system.
