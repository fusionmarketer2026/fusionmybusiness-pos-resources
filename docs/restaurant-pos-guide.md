# Restaurant POS System: An Introduction

A restaurant point-of-sale (POS) system helps food businesses manage sales transactions and day-to-day service operations.

## Common capabilities to evaluate

When comparing restaurant POS systems, businesses commonly assess:

- Order entry and order management
- Payment processing
- Menu and item management
- Receipts and transaction records
- Sales reporting
- Staff access and permissions
- Hardware and peripheral compatibility

The capabilities available vary by provider and product configuration. Confirm features directly with the provider before making a purchasing decision.
